> Save time. Apply smarter. Track everything.

---

## Strategy: Quality Over Volume

Don't spray and pray. Target 5–10 strong applications per week over 50 weak ones.

| Tier | Effort | Expected Response Rate |
|------|--------|----------------------|
| Tier 1 (customized, referral) | 30 min each | 30–50% |
| Tier 2 (tailored cover, applied direct) | 15 min each | 10–20% |
| Tier 3 (quick apply, LinkedIn Easy Apply) | 2 min each | 2–5% |

## Resume ATS Optimization

Most companies use ATS (Applicant Tracking System) to filter before a human reads.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Use keywords from the job description in your resume
- [ ] No tables, columns, or images in ATS-submitted resume
- [ ] Use standard section headers: "Experience", "Skills", "Education"
- [ ] Save as PDF unless they ask for Word
- [ ] Run through [JobScan](https://www.jobscan.co) — aim for 70%+ match

## Weekly Application Routine

```
Monday:    Identify 10 target roles (LinkedIn, Naukri, Wellfound)
Tuesday:   Apply to 5 Tier-1/2 roles with tailored resume
Wednesday: Follow up on previous week's applications
Thursday:  Cold outreach to 2–3 HRs/recruiters
Friday:    Update Tracker, review what worked
```
