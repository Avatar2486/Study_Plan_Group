## 1. Tell Me About Yourself

> This is your opening pitch. Keep it to 60–75 seconds.

I'm a Full Stack Developer with 3+ years of experience at Boson Technology in Mumbai. My core stack is Angular, Node.js, and Python — and I've been heavily involved in AI integration work over the last 2 years.

Some highlights: I built an AI translation system that generates $200–400 in daily revenue, automated workflows that save $75K/year in costs, and a Twilio-based platform handling 1,000+ concurrent calls. ## 2. Why Are You Leaving Your Current Company?

**Answer (Safe & Honest):**
I've had a genuinely good run at Boson Technology — I've built real production systems, worked with AI, and taken on end-to-end ownership. But after 3 years, I've reached a point where the growth curve has flattened. I want to work on larger teams, on more complex challenges, and step into a senior engineering role. That's something this opportunity offers.

**What NOT to say:** Don't mention salary first, don't criticize the company or management.

- With AI/ML skills + revenue impact: ₹18–28 LPA is realistic

**Answer:**
Based on my research and the scope of this role, I'm looking in the range of **₹18–25 LPA**. But I'm flexible — the right role, team, and growth trajectory matter more to me than just the number. What's the budget for this position?

## 8. Tell Me About a Challenging Project

Use the **STAR method**: Situation → Task → Action → Result

**Example (Translation System):**
- **S:** Company needed to translate 500+ CSV files weekly across 50+ languages — being done manually.
- **T:** Build an automated pipeline that's accurate, fast, and scalable.
- **A:** Integrated HuggingFace MBart-50 with batch processing, Flask API, MySQL storage on GCP.
- **R:** 95% accuracy, 500+ files/week processed, generating $200–400/day in revenue.

---

## 9. Do You Have Any Questions for Us?

**Always ask 2–3 questions. This shows you're serious.**

1. What does the engineering team's current tech stack look like, and what are the biggest pain points you're trying to solve?
2. How does the team handle technical debt vs. new feature development?
3. What does a successful first 90 days look like for this role?
4. Is there a defined path from this role to a senior/lead position?

---

## 10. Tell Me About a Time You Handled Conflict

**Answer (STAR):**
- **S:** A backend integration was blocked because the API team had a different expectation about the data format.
- **T:** Ship the feature without delaying the frontend work.
- **A:** I documented both sides, proposed a contract-first API spec, and got both teams aligned in one call.
- **R:** Shipped on time, and the spec doc became our team standard.
