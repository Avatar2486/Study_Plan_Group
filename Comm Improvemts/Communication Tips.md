# Communication Improvement Guide

> Simple, practical tips. Read once, practice daily.

---

## STAR Method (For Any Story in an Interview)

Use this for every behavioral question: *"Tell me about a time when..."*

| Step | What to Say |
|------|-------------|
| **S**ituation | Set the context in 1 sentence |
| **T**ask | What was your responsibility |
| **A**ction | What YOU specifically did (use "I", not "we") |
| **R**esult | Numbers, impact, outcome |

**Example:** "Our invoice process took 2 hours daily (S). My task was to automate it (T). I built a Gmail-to-S3 pipeline with Gemini AI (A). It now takes 36 minutes — 70% faster (R)."

---

## Email Communication

### Do:
- Subject line = action needed (e.g., "Interview Follow-Up — Abhishek Dubey")
- First sentence = context (who you are + why you're writing)
- Keep under 100 words for cold outreach
- End with a clear ask or next step

### Don't:
- Start with "Hope this email finds you well"
- Write walls of text
- Use passive voice ("It was decided..." → "I decided...")

---

## Phone/Video Call Tips

- **First 30 seconds:** State your name + why you're calling
- **Pace:** Slow down when explaining technical things
- **Pause:** After answering, stop. Don't fill silence.
- **Confirm:** End each topic with "Does that make sense?" or "Happy to go deeper if helpful"
- **Camera:** Eye contact = look at the camera, not the screen

---

## Technical Explanation (To Non-Technical People)

Use analogies. Example:
- "n8n automation is like a recipe — when this happens, do that"
- "Nginx is the doorman of the server — it decides where each request goes"
- "JWT is like a hotel key card — once issued, it proves who you are without asking the front desk each time"

---

## Vocabulary Upgrades

| Instead of... | Say... |
|---------------|--------|
| I did X | I owned / architected / engineered / led X |
| It was good | It resulted in [metric] |
| I fixed a bug | I resolved a [performance/security/reliability] issue |
| I made a feature | I shipped / delivered / built X that [impact] |
| I worked with the team | I collaborated across [X] teams to [outcome] |

---

## Confidence Boosters Before Interviews

1. Read [[About Me/Profile]] — remind yourself of your numbers
2. Say your "Tell me about yourself" answer out loud once
3. Write down 3 things you're proud of from the last year
4. Dress well — even for video calls
