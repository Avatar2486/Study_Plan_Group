# Study Log

> One entry per session. Keep it short — what you covered, what stuck, what to revisit.
> Helps you see your streak and pick up exactly where you left off.

---

## How to log a session

Copy this block and fill it in:

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — Topic Name
- **Covered:** (what sections/questions you went through)
- **Understood:** (what clicked — 1–2 lines)
- **Confused about:** (what to revisit next time)
- **Next session:** (exact section to start from)
- **Time spent:** X mins
```
