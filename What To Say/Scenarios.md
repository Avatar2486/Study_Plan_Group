# What To Say — Scripts & Scenarios

> Copy-paste ready scripts for real situations. Short, direct, professional.

---

## Salary Negotiation

### When They Ask Your Number First
> "Based on my research for this role in [city/remote], and given my experience with Angular, Node.js, and AI integration — I'm targeting ₹18–25 LPA. What's the range you've budgeted for this position?"

### When Their Offer Is Too Low
> "Thank you for the offer — I'm genuinely excited about the role. The number is a bit below what I was expecting based on my research. I was targeting closer to ₹X. Is there flexibility there?"

### Counter Offer (Written/Email)
> "Thank you for the offer letter. I've reviewed it carefully and I'm very interested in joining. I wanted to discuss the base compensation — I was expecting ₹X based on the role scope and my experience. Could we revisit that figure?"

### When They Can't Move on Salary
> "I understand. Can we discuss other components — joining bonus, performance review timeline, variable payout, or remote work flexibility? That would help bridge the gap."

## Notice Period Negotiation

### Asking for Early Relieving (to your manager)
> "I've received an offer that requires me to join in [X weeks]. I've really valued my time here and want to ensure a smooth handover. Could we discuss an early relieving arrangement?"

### Telling the New Company About Notice Period
> "My standard notice period is 2 months, but I'm actively working with my current employer to accelerate this. I'm optimistic we can get closer to [4–6 weeks]. Can we keep that timeline flexible while I confirm?"

## Receiving a Job Offer

### Buying Time (Don't accept on the call)
> "This is great news — I'm excited about this opportunity. I'd love to review the offer letter in full before formally accepting. Could you send it over? I'll get back to you within 2 days."

### Accepting an Offer
> "I've reviewed the offer letter and I'm happy to accept. Please let me know the next steps — background check, documentation, etc."

### Declining an Offer Politely
> "Thank you so much for the offer — I genuinely appreciated the process and your team's time. After careful consideration, I've decided to pursue a different opportunity that aligns more closely with my current goals. I hope we can stay in touch."

## Responding to Recruiters (LinkedIn/Email)

### When You're Interested
> "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! The role sounds interesting. Could you share more about the tech stack, team size, and the CTC range? Happy to connect for a call if it's a good fit."

### When You're Not Interested Right Now
> "Hi [Name], thanks for thinking of me. I'm not actively looking right now, but I'd be open to staying in touch. Could you share the JD anyway — I might know someone who'd be a great fit."

---

## During the Interview

### When You Don't Know the Answer
> "That's a good question. I haven't worked directly with [X], but my approach would be to [logical reasoning]. I'd also want to look at [documentation/resource] to validate that. Would that kind of problem-solving approach work for your team?"

### When the Interview Is Going Long
> "I want to be mindful of your time. Should I wrap up on this topic, or would you like me to go deeper?"

### At the End — Expressing Interest
> "I want to say — after this conversation, I'm genuinely more interested than before. The challenges you're working on align well with what I enjoy building. What are the next steps in the process?"
