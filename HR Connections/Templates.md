## LinkedIn — Cold Outreach to HR/Recruiter

**Short Connection Note (300 char limit):**
> Hi [Name], I'm a Full Stack Developer (3+ yrs, Angular/Node/AI) at Boson Technology, Mumbai. Actively exploring senior roles. Would love to connect — happy to share my profile if relevant!

**After Connection — First Message:**
> Hi [Name], thanks for connecting! I'm exploring [type of role] roles currently. I have 3+ years building production systems — Angular, Node.js, Python, AI integrations. Would you be open to a quick call or sharing relevant openings? Here's my [LinkedIn/resume link].

## Asking for Feedback After Rejection

> Hi [Name], thanks for letting me know. I respect the decision. Would you be open to sharing brief feedback on where I could improve? It would genuinely help me grow. No pressure — appreciate your time either way.
